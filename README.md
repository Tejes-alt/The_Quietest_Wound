## 🩸 About

**The Quietest Wound** is a historical psychological novella about a man who spends his life believing that emotional emptiness is strength.

He becomes a soldier.

Then a conqueror.

Then something feared enough to move armies.

War teaches him to suppress hesitation, empathy, guilt, and attachment. He learns to see humanity through **power, fear, hierarchy, and survival**.

For years, he believes he has mastered the thing that makes people weak:

**feeling.**

Then he meets her.

Not on a peaceful road.
Not under beautiful circumstances.

He finds her in the aftermath of a fallen kingdom, carrying a lantern through the ruins and treating wounded soldiers without caring which side they belonged to.

While he has spent his entire life learning how to destroy,

**she has spent hers learning how to care.**

And that difference begins to undo him.

---

## ⚔️ The Story

The novella opens on a battlefield.

At sixteen, the narrator kills his first man.

By nineteen, death no longer affects him.

People call it:

**Composure.**  
**Discipline.**  
**Control.**

He knows it as something else.

**Emotional starvation.**

His transformation into a feared warrior reaches its darkest point during the massacre of Velmora and the fall of Ardenhal. Surrounded by destruction, he realizes that he has become emotionally untouched by suffering.

Then, at the edge of death, he sees her.

She is helping the wounded.

Enemy soldiers.

His soldiers.

It does not matter to her.

She simply believes that suffering is worth answering.

And for the first time, something reaches him.

---

## 🌧️ When Violence Meets Kindness

Their relationship does not begin with romance.

It begins with silence.

She treats his wounds.

They eat soup together.

They sit beneath lanternlight without needing to speak.

Days become weeks.

Weeks become months.

And somewhere within that quiet routine, the man who once believed attachment was weakness begins waiting for her footsteps.

Then a snake enters the room.

For the first time in his life, fear moves through him before thought does.

Not fear for himself.

**Fear of losing someone else.**

That is the moment he realizes something has changed.

> *"For the first time in my life, I had been afraid to lose someone."*

---

## 🕯️ Becoming Human

The story is ultimately not about war.

It is about what remains **after war**.

The narrator enters Elaris expecting to survive.

Instead, he discovers ordinary life.

Children laughing.

Farmers repairing fences.

People helping strangers.

Rain against windows.

Quiet mornings.

Work that has no glory attached to it.

For the first time, he begins to understand that there is a kind of strength that cannot be measured by how many people fear you.

There is strength in kindness.

There is strength in vulnerability.

There is strength in remaining human.

## 💭 The Heart of the Story

The narrator once believed that emotions made people weak.

She teaches him something different.

That feeling deeply is not weakness.

That caring can require more courage than killing.

That survival is not the same thing as living.

And that some people enter our lives not to stay beside us forever, but to quietly change the direction of who we become.

## ✍️ About the Author

**Tes**

A writer exploring the intersection of **human nature, psychology, philosophy, emotion, and storytelling**.

*The Quietest Wound* examines what happens when a person spends an entire lifetime trying to become incapable of feeling — and eventually discovers that feeling was the part of himself he needed most.
